# Mini AN4 ESPnet3 integration recipes

Mini AN4 contains small CI fixtures for the ESPnet3 ASR, classification, and speech translation systems. They exercise complete stage pipelines on a handful of examples; their models and scores are not intended as benchmarks.

- [ASR](esp2_asr/README.md)
- [Classification](esp2_cls/README.md)
- [Speech translation](esp2_st/README.md)
